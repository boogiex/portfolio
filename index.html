About

Commercial judgement. Technical depth.

I’m a martech and growth professional working across platform strategy, analytics and experimentation. At the Financial Times, I lead the AI Paywall workstream, setting strategy for how the model supports commercial growth.

My work connects business objectives with the platforms, data and customer experiences that deliver them. That includes evaluating vendors, owning product roadmaps for marketing teams, modelling customer value and leading experimentation from prioritisation through to delivery and analysis. I connect technical optimisation with acquisition, conversion and retention, helping teams understand how product priorities support consumer growth.

An engineering degree gave me the technical foundation I developed at In Digital, working with publishers and B2C businesses on integrations, measurement and subscription growth. I bring that hands-on understanding to commercial decisions: what to invest in, how to measure value and where to improve the customer journey.
